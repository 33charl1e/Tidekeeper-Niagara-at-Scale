# Reference study notes

These notes are based on published game descriptions. They do not record a
personal play or trailer-viewing session. Before submitting the proposal, view
the official trailers on the linked store pages and add your own observations.

## Sheepherds!

The published description centers on sheepdogs guiding flocks through a landscape.
Tidekeeper borrows indirect flock control through the position and influence of
the player-controlled beacon. Its timed rescue objective is this project's own
rule; the reference's description emphasizes relaxed play without a time limit.

[Official Steam page and trailers](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3006280/Sheepherds/)

Look for how distance and movement communicate pressure on a flock. Record one
specific moment from the trailer or your play session and how it informed a rule.

## ABZU

The publisher's store description describes thousands of fish responding to the
player, each other and predators. Tidekeeper uses that as a visual and simulation
reference for the large GPU school, while keeping a much smaller, top-down arena.

[Official Steam page and trailers](https://store.steampowered.com/app/384190/ABZU/)

Look for readable group motion and the balance between individual fish and the
school's overall shape. Record one specific observation after viewing.
